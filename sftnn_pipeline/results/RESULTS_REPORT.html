Experiment #7 is the final SFTNN — thesis and live demo

Five SFTNN configurations and three rejected mechanisms were tested against a fixed, bootstrap-verified protocol before this decision was made. This page documents the full experimental arc, why each prior configuration was set aside, and the complete result set for the one that was kept.

Final decision

Thesis and live demo both use the Experiment #7 checkpoint. No separate model for the demonstration — the same weights evaluated in the results chapter are the ones presented live.

The experimental arc

Two data corrections came first and are adopted throughout everything below. Three region-aware training mechanisms were then tried and rejected before the numbered experiment sequence began. Five numbered SFTNN configurations followed, each changing exactly one thing from the last.

DATA FIXES

Southeast Asia label-completeness fix + funding-disclosure indicator

SEA success rate 26.6% → 38.0% after excluding 210 rows with a source-side missing-data artifact; added a binary flag distinguishing "not disclosed" from "raised $0," concentrated in Africa/South Asia.
Adopted — both are corrective, not tuned, and precede every experiment below.
REJECTED
MECHANISMS

Group DRO · per-region threshold tuning · per-region γ/β modulation ceiling

Three different ways of giving minority regions more explicit weight in training or inference.
All reverted — Group DRO regressed aggregate ROC-AUC/F1/Recall; per-region thresholds reduced aggregate Recall/F1 and erased a South Asia advantage; the γ/β ceiling collapsed shrinkage to ~0.001 and dropped SEA's ROC-AUC from 0.814 to 0.710.
ORIGINAL

Plain validation ROC-AUC as the Optuna objective

embed_dim=4, shrink_lambda=6.245. Genuine, ablation-confirmed region-specific differentiation for North America and Latin America — the one configuration where that mechanism visibly worked as designed.
Not final — wins zero aggregate metrics and zero raw per-region metrics in either of the two regions where it shows genuine differentiation; South Asia is its only real per-region win.
EXP #4

Composite validation objective introduced

0.35·AUC + 0.25·PR-AUC + 0.25·F1 + 0.15·macro-Recall, applied identically to all four models. First aggregate win (Recall, 0.757). But shrink_lambda collapsed to 0.00625 (~1000× down), and Southeast Asia (F1 −0.062) and Latin America (F1 −0.049) both regressed.
Not final — the aggregate win was bought at the cost of the two regions the thesis prioritizes most.
EXP #5

Shrinkage floor raised (0.3–10), formula unchanged

A direct attempt to undo #4's collapse by constraining the search range. shrink_lambda=1.914 landed well clear of the floor — but Southeast Asia stayed fully flattened and Latin America's genuine Original-era differentiation was destroyed, not restored.
Not final — the intervention worked exactly as designed and still didn't fix the regions it targeted. Diagnosed, not just observed: a confound was found in how the penalty scales with embed_dim.
EXP #6

Shrinkage penalty corrected: mean, not sum, over embedding dimensions

Fixed the confound found in #5 (penalty scaled ~4× between embed_dim=4 and 16); rescaled the search range to 1.0–50.0 as the mandatory companion change. Latin America's F1 numerically recovered to 0.645 — but the ablation showed this was illusory, still fully flattened, not genuine. Southeast Asia's mechanism became actively harmful (−0.031 Recall drop) for the first time.
Not final — the mathematically correct fix, tested honestly, made Southeast Asia mechanistically worse, not better.
EXP #7 →
FINAL

Capacity search restricted to #4's larger configurations, #6's fixes kept

hidden_dim∈{64,128}, n_layers∈{2,3} (32 and 1 dropped), mean-based shrinkage, composite objective, all unchanged otherwise. Result below.
Selected — bootstrap-robust aggregate F1 win (vs. TabNet 82.5%, XGBoost 74.3%); South Asia's mechanism upgraded from mildly harmful to neutral with a decisive bootstrap improvement (3W–2T–1L → 5W–1T–0L); Southeast Asia and Latin America performed identically to #5 — equal, not worse.

Why #7 over #5, specifically

#5 and #7 share the same architecture, objective, and shrinkage formula, differing only in the capacity search space. The decision came down to a direct, region-by-region comparison on the thesis's actual regions of interest — not aggregate F1, which was deliberately set aside as the deciding factor.

Deciding logic

On the three regions the thesis is actually built around — Southeast Asia, South Asia, Latin America — #7 is equal to #5 in two and clearly better in the third. The only region where #5 leads is East Asia, a supplementary 7th region outside the original six the methodology's Table 4.7 is structured around. #5 does not provide materially stronger evidence for underrepresented regions; by the pre-agreed decision rule, that makes #7 the correct choice independent of its aggregate F1 win.

The claim this evidence actually supports

What to write:

“The final SFTNN demonstrated competitive overall startup success prediction while providing explicit region-aware modeling. Its regional conditioning was particularly beneficial in South Asia, while its effects were neutral or varied across other underrepresented regions.”

What not to write: “SFTNN outperforms all other models in underrepresented regions.” It doesn't — Southeast Asia and Latin America show flattened, not genuine, conditioning in every configuration tested, including the final one. The claim above is narrower, and it is the one the ablation and bootstrap tables actually back.

Standing limitations, still true for Exp #7

  • Small per-region test sets. Africa n=24, Southeast Asia n=76, Latin America n=105, South Asia n=111 — report bootstrap intervals, not bare point estimates.
  • Proxy labels. Minority regions rely more heavily on the age/funding-round heuristic than North America does, tracking their lower base success rates.
  • Region composition. Singapore is ~48% of the Southeast Asia bucket.
  • Inert-to-mixed conditioning. Only North America shows genuine, ablation-confirmed differentiation in every one of the five SFTNN configurations tested this session.
  • Single split. Bootstrap quantifies test-set sampling variance only, not train/val/test partition variance.
